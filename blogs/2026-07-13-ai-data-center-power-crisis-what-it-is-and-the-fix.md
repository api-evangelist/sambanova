Delivering on the promise of AI is no longer constrained by the technology that enables it, but by the ability of modern data centers to power the systems it runs on. The result is a growing gap between what the most advanced AI systems demand and what today's facilities can actually deliver. TL;DR: The AI data center power crisis is the growing mismatch between the power next-generation AI systems require and what existing data centers can supply.
